DEEP LEARNING & ViT FEATURE EXTRACTION IN CHEST RADIOGRAPHY
================================================================================

1. VISION TRANSFORMER (ViT) MECHANICS IN X-RAY ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Objective: Map how self-attention mechanisms interpret lung pathology compared to human radiologists.

A. PATCH EMBEDDING AND FLATTENING
- Mechanism: The ViT divides the chest radiograph into fixed-size grids (e.g., 16x16 or 32x32 pixels), flattening them into 1D sequences. 

- Significance: Unlike Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) that use sliding local windows, ViTs evaluate the relationship of *every patch to every other patch* simultaneously.
- Clinical Translation: This global context allows the model to immediately recognize symmetric bilateral diseases (common in viral infections) or isolated asymmetric densities (common in bacterial infections).

B. FEATURE EXTRACTION BY TISSUE TYPE
- Consolidation (Bacterial): The ViT assigns extremely high self-attention weights to contiguous patches that display uniform high pixel density (brightness) and an absence of standard lung tissue frequency (loss of vascular shadows).
- Ground-Glass Opacities (Viral): The ViT detects GGOs by identifying patches with elevated pixel intensity that *still maintain* the structural frequency of the lung's vascular grid.


2. ETIOLOGY DIFFERENTIATION: MODEL ATTENTION PATTERNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Objective: Correlate spatial attention mapping with specific pathogens.

A. BACTERIAL PNEUMONIA ATTENTION MAPS
- Spatial Distribution: Highly focal, asymmetric, and localized.
- Visual Output: The attention heatmap heavily highlights a single lobe or specific segment (lobar consolidation), showing intense, clustered activation while ignoring the rest of the lung field.

- Pathogen Link: Correlates strongly with S. pneumoniae and K. pneumoniae presentations.

B. VIRAL PNEUMONIA ATTENTION MAPS
- Spatial Distribution: Diffuse, scattered, bilateral, and peripheral.
- Visual Output: The attention heatmap shows widespread activation, particularly drawn to the outer edges of both lungs and the lower zones. 

- Pathogen Link: Maps perfectly to the clinical presentation of interstitial viral pneumonias, such as COVID-19 or Influenza.


3. EXPLAINABILITY & BIAS MITIGATION ("THE BLACK BOX")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary Objective: Ensure the ViT is predicting based on lung parenchyma, not artifactual confounders.

A. THE OVERFITTING RISK
- Deep learning models can achieve high accuracy by learning artifactual features rather than biological ones. 
- Examples: 
  1. Correlating portable X-ray machine text markers with severe ICU viral cases.
  2. Correlating patient positioning (supine vs. erect) with specific etiologies.
  3. Locking onto chest tubes, ECG leads, or pacemakers instead of lung tissue.

B. ATTENTION HEATMAP VALIDATION (LangGraph / Grad-CAM)
- Mechanism: Extracting the class attention map of the final [CLS] token to visualize exactly which patches drove the ViT's prediction.
- Validation Rule: A prediction is only clinically valid if the highest-weighted attention patches fall squarely within the lung parenchyma, mapping directly to recognized features (consolidation, GGOs, infiltrates).
